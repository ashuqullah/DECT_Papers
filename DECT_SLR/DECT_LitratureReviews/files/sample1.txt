urations presented in Section IV, we conducted Monte Carlo
experiments and measured the Bit Error Rate (BER) and
Packet Error Rate (PER) at the receiver. Each experiment ran
until a convergence at a PER of 10e−5 or less was obtained,
as this is the threshold that according to [2] has to be reached
for the URLLC use case.
