                             VI. C ONCLUSION

    While 5G and future 6G deployments provide highly accu-
 rate and ultra low latency timing for command and control data
 required for the tactile internet, including robotics and meta-
 verse applications, this performance comes at a high cost for
 deployment. Our measurements indicate that latency values as
 well as packet delay variations, i.e., jitter, are all approaching
 the thresholds required for tactile internet applications, such
 as robotics or the metaverse.
    Future evaluations will target overall performance measure-
 ments including sustainable data rates. Further research will
 target potentials for further latency reductions and combining
 DECT-2020 NR+ with other approaches to derive a complete
 system implementation.
