                                                                                    rate across various scenarios. The results indicate that LoRa
                                                                                    in the 2.4 GHz band, due to its signiﬁcant range, is particularly
                                                                                    well-suited for applications requiring reliable long-distance
                                                                                    communication, such as reading and controlling water meters,
                                                                                    as well as transmitting telemetry data for emergency services

                                                                                    LoRa for scenarios prioritizing robustness and range, while
                                                                                    DECT NR+ serves as a valuable alternative for conﬁned areas
                                                                                    beneﬁting from high data rates.
