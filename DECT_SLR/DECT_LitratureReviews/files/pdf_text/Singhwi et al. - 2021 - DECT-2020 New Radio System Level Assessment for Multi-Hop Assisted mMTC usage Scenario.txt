      This paper has carried out the simulations over DECT-2020
   NR specifications for the Urban-Macro mMTC scenario. Based
   on the channel/frequency assignments, proximity of devices
   and relay (RDFT,PT ) selection, we proposed the different ap-
   proaches to avoid the interference by numerous interfering
   devices in a massive connecting Urban-Macro environment as
   follows.
      • Angles approach: selection of equidistant RDFT,PT s,
      • Farthest distance approach: channel assignment based on
        RDFT,PT s that are farthest from each other,
      • Nearest distance approach: channel assignment based on
        RDFT,PT s that are nearest to each other, and
      • Random approach: random selection of RDFT,PT s and
        channel assignment.
      Rest of the paper is organized as follows. Section II de-
   scribes the system model of massive connecting devices over
   DECT-2020 NR specifications. Section III provides the CDF
   analysis of such a system. Section IV describes the simulation                             Fig. 1: Illustration of multi-hop DECT model [9]
   results, and the paper is concluded in section V.
