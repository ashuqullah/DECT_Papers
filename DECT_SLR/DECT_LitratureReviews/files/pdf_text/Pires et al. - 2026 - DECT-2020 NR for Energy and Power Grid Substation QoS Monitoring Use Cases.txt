      In the realm of power grid communications, IEC 61850 is                         machine-type communication (mMTC) use cases. Its mesh
                                                                                      topology is a key feature, due to its self-healing capabilities
                                                                                      and its decentralized topology. As shown in Figure 1 mesh
                                                                                      operation is based on a clustered tree structure, where Radio
                                                                                      Devices (RDs) dynamically select upstream nodes using beacon
                                                                                      information and routing cost metrics. Devices can operate in
                                                                                      Fixed Termination (FT) mode, with backend connectivity acting
                                                                                      as Sinks, or in Portable Termination (PT) mode associating to
                                                                                      FT mode devices, or in both FT and PT mode simultaneously,
                                                                                      forming multi-hop paths to extend coverage. Cluster formation
                                                                                      occurs hop-by-hop, with each RD, in PT mode, associating to
                                                                                      the most suitable parent RD, operating as FT, based on link
                                                                                      quality and cost. This decentralized approach supports high
                                                                                      device densities and robust connectivity in interference-prone
                                                                                      environments while extending the network coverage [10].
