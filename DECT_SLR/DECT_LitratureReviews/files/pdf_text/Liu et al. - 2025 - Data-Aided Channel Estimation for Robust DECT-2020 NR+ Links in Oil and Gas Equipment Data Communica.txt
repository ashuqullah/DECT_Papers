                         V. C ONCLUSION
    A data-aided LS channel estimation scheme is presented for
 DECT-2020 NR+ tailored to oil and gas equipment data com-
 munication. By augmenting pilot-based LS with reliability-
 gated decoded data, the receiver achieves lower CTF MSE
 and improved decoding performance under low SNR and
 frequency-selective fading, without increasing pilot overhead
 or computational burden. Simulations on AWGN and TDL
 channels confirm consistent gains and practical deployability
 in metallic, multipath-rich oil and gas environments. Future
 work includes fusion with adaptive MMSE/Kalman filters,
 MIMO extensions for spatial diversity around large equipment,
 and field trials in live refinery networks to quantify end-to-
 end reliability and energy benefits. In oil and gas operations,
 these improvements directly support more reliable telemetry
 for safety interlocks and condition-based maintenance, even
 amid rotating machinery and heavy steel infrastructure. The
 approach also aligns with the sector’s power and spectrum
 constraints, enabling longer sensor lifetimes and denser mesh
 deployments without sacrificing link robustness.
