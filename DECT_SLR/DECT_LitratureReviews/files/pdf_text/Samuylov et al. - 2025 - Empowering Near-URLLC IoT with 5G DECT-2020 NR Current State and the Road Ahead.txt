            5G DECT-2020 Technology
The DECT-2020 NR physical layer was designed
to benefit from recent development in radio
technologies and still allow for compatible low-
cost implementation. To avoid reliance on local
spectrum regulators, the utilized frequency bands
are the DECT bands, 1880–1900 MHz band in
Europe and Australia, and 1910–1920 MHz band
in North America, supporting up to 11 or 5 chan-
nels, respectively, with a bandwidth of 1.728 MHz
each. Other bands are also supported including
those in the sub-GHz region [7]. Coupled with dif-
ferent emitted power allowed for devices, DECT-
2020 can cover large territories.                                FIGURE 1. Illustration of DECT-2020 network formation.

            Novel Data Dissemination Methods
Meeting URLLC constraints in wireless mesh net-
works remains an unresolved challenge. One pos-
sible solution is to use network coding, which is
typically applied at higher layers in the network
stack. It enhances end-to-end communication reli-
ability and reduces the latency caused by multi-
hop scenarios using linear algebra over finite
fields [12]. With this approach, data of end-to-end
nodes are received at intermediate nodes, decod-
ed and used to construct a specific composite
data signals (e.g., by XOR operation). These sig-
nals are then forwarded toward the destination
nodes. By combining multiple received signals the
destination node can extract the data intended
for it. In the design of the network coding, it is
worth noting that URLLC constraints are usually
imposed only on the communication between
a limited number of nodes within a small area.
Therefore, in most cases it is sufficient to apply
the network coding to the intra-cluster communi-
cation. In some use-cases even a topology agnos-
tic network coding is applicable, avoiding any
signaling overhead for topology changes.
    As DECT-2020 operates on a TDMA-based
system, network coding can optimize scheduling
time slots, reducing the total number of transmis-
sion between devices and so reducing their ener-
gy consumption. For this purpose, methods for
constructing this composite signal are needed,
focusing on seamless integration into the protocol
stack. For an effective implementation, informing
RDs about network coding-assisted communica-
tion via MAC protocols is necessary.
