Summary:

This paper addresses the central research challenge of the selected DECT-2020 NR / 5G / wireless networking domain. It focuses on how the proposed design or algorithm improves reliability, coverage, resource management, or performance for emerging URLLC, mMTC, or mesh-networked applications.

Problem and motivation:
—Oil and gas extraction and processing environments impose stringent
requirements on wireless data links used for equipment monitoring, control, and
safety. Multipath-rich metallic structures, Radio Frequency(RF) blocking, and
dynamic interference degrade channel estimation accuracy, especially at low SNR.
This paper adapts and evaluates a data-aided least-squares (LS) channel
estimation approach—originally developed and validated for Digital Enhancement
Cordless Telecommunication2020 New Radio Plus(DECT-2020 NR+)—to oil and gas
equipment data communication over Orthogonal Frequency Division
Multiplexing(OFDM) links. By combining pilot symbols with previously decoded
data symbols, the proposed receiver improves channel transfer function (CTF)
estimates without increasing pilot overhead. MATLAB simulations on AWGN and 3GPP
TDL channels, coupled with a lab validation using a 979-8-3315-7017-0/25/$31.00
©2025 IEEE DECT development board and a backscatter-like test signal, show
consistent MSE gains over pilot-only LS across SNRs. Deployment aspects for oil
and gas sites, including subcarrier numerology, packetization, and frequency
planning in the 1.9 GHz frequency band, which is used in some industrial
wireless systems. Detailed implementation guidance is provided for field
gateways and sensor Radio Devices(RDs). Results indicate that data-aided
estimation enhances reliability and spectral efficiency for real-time equipment
telemetry and alarm reporting in harsh oilfield environments.
