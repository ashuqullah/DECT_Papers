Summary:

This paper addresses the central research challenge of the selected DECT-2020 NR / 5G / wireless networking domain. It focuses on how the proposed design or algorithm improves reliability, coverage, resource management, or performance for emerging URLLC, mMTC, or mesh-networked applications.

Problem and motivation:
—This paper addresses efficient positioning and user device tracking in
Internet-of-Things (IoT) networks with particular focus on the new DECT-2020 New
Radio standard – the first non-cellular 5G technology standard in the world.
Stemming from fundamental performance requirements of IoT networks, for example,
related to energy consumption, latency, and reliability, it is important to
utilize available radio resources efficiently, while avoiding redundant
transmissions and signaling. In this paper, we propose a tracking-based
positioning solution with a proactive measurement selection, in which
positioning measurements are selected based on Fisher information. The proposed
method, accompanied with computationally attractive processing solution, is able
to sort and select potential positioning measurements based on achievable
minimum positioning error. The provided numerical results, considering both
range and angle measurements, show that the proposed method outperforms the
traditional signal-tonoise ratio based measurement selection approach, and
enables positioning with a smaller number of measurements.

NTRODUCTION To accommodate growing demand on Internet-of-Things (IoT) based
services, the new Digital Enhanced Cordless Telecommunications - 2020 New Radio
(DECT-2020 NR) [1], [2] was introduced as part of the official International
Telecommunication Union (ITU) fifth generation (5G) standards in 2022 [3]. The
DECT-2020 NR is the first non-cellular 5G technology, designed especially for
IoT use cases with support for 5G performance requirements regarding ultra
reliable low latency communications (URLLC) and massive machine type
communications (mMTC). Positioning plays an important role in various IoT and
wireless sensor network (WSN) systems, as well as related applications and
location-based services (LBS) [4]. DECT-2020 NRbased positioning is not yet
widely studied due to its technological novelty, but research works focusing on
positioning with other IoT systems, or IoT-like scenarios, can be found, for
example, in [4]–[9]. In [4], different IoT-based positioning approaches are
compared, and classified in a IoT infrastructure applicable framework, while
also considering aspects of power consumption and energy efficiency.
Furthermore, possible positioning methods for IoT systems are comprehensively
discussed in [5], [9], including also comparison between positioning
capabilities of different IoT technologies.

The work contributes practical insight for DECT-2020 NR and related next-generation wireless systems. It provides experimental or simulated evidence for the proposed scheme and highlights how the results support stricter reliability, lower latency, or improved resource utilization in private network or industrial IoT scenarios.
