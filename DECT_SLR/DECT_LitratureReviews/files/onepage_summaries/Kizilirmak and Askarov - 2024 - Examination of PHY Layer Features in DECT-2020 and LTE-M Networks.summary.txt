Title: Surabaya, Indonesia, November 6 – 8, 2024

Summary:

This paper addresses the central research challenge of the selected DECT-2020 NR / 5G / wireless networking domain. It focuses on how the proposed design or algorithm improves reliability, coverage, resource management, or performance for emerging URLLC, mMTC, or mesh-networked applications.

Problem and motivation:
—This paper provides a comparative analysis of the physical layer (PHY)
characteristics of DECT-2020 NR and LTE-M, two critical standards for IoT
communication. DECT-2020 NR, developed by ETSI, is intended for industrial IoT
applications and provides low-latency, reliable communication in high-density
environments. In contrast, LTE-M, which is a member of the 3GPP family, is
designed to provide broad coverage, energy efﬁciency, and mobility support for
low-power devices. The study emphasizes the distinctions between the standards
regarding data rates, modulation schemes, and frequency bands, displaying how
each standard fulﬁlls speciﬁc IoT requirements. Furthermore, it highlights the
complementing characteristics of DECT-2020 NR and LTE-M, by providing
performance insights such as bit-error rate (BER) analysis and spectral
efﬁciency.
