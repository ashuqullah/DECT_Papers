Summary:

This paper addresses the central research challenge of the selected DECT-2020 NR / 5G / wireless networking domain. It focuses on how the proposed design or algorithm improves reliability, coverage, resource management, or performance for emerging URLLC, mMTC, or mesh-networked applications.

Problem and motivation:
Many emerging applications require a higher level of flexibility, modularity,
and efficiency but are dependent on advancements in communication infrastructure
and distributed computing. Time-sensitive networking (TSN) standards aim at
providing vendor agnostic, reliable, and deterministic communications over the
Ethernet, but lack in flexibility and modularity provisions. In this context
wireless communication systems are preferred given the obvious benefits in terms
of increased flexibility, reduced deployment & maintenance costs, and inherent
mobility support. However, the stochastic nature of the wireless medium poses
several challenges in achieving these benefits. In this paper we comprehensively
analyze the recent standardization efforts and developments in IEEE 802.11 and
5G to enable low-latency, deterministic communications and present the current
status of their integration with wired TSN. Then, we present a set of use cases
that may be enabled by wireless TSN including industrial automation, automotive,
or audiovisual applications.

Approach and methods:
The development of future information technology (IT) and operational technology
(OT) is being promoted by the vision of flexible, enhanced intelligence, and
modular networks. The communication requirements for various applications are
both demanding and diverse [1]. As an example, the growing complexity in
industrial sensing and closed-loop control systems require the use of
heterogeneous sensors, actuators, and control units that have to operate in a
predictable and synchronous manner. The elements of the control system can be
distributed over short or long distances, and thus the use of communication
networks is compulsory to obtain a costeffective solution. Additionally, there
is a high demand on the degree of flexibility provided by the communication
network given the increasing number of mobile devices. On the one hand, the
wired communication solutions that provide deterministic latency and high
reliability may require cumbersome and expensive cabling or may not be even
suitable due to lack of functionality such as mobility [2], [3]. On the other
hand, the current state of wireless solutions do not provide the deterministic
latency and high reliability required for emerging use cases. The time-sensitive

Contributions and implications:
The work contributes practical insight for DECT-2020 NR and related next-generation wireless systems. It provides experimental or simulated evidence for the proposed scheme and highlights how the results support stricter reliability, lower latency, or improved resource utilization in private network or industrial IoT scenarios.
